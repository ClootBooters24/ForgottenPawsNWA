Become a Volunteer

Join our 100% volunteer-run organization and make a real difference in the lives of forgotten dogs. We're always looking for dedicated individuals to help with care, socialization, transportation, and more.

Volunteer Opportunities

Dog Care & Socialization

Help with feeding, walking, play time, and socializing our dogs to prepare them for their forever homes.

Dog Transportation

Help transport dogs to vet appointments, fostering homes, and adoption events.

Event Support

Assist at adoption events, fundraisers, and community outreach programs.

Administrative Help

Support with social media, data entry, grant writing, and general office tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience with dogs?

No! We welcome volunteers of all experience levels. We'll provide training and guidance for all volunteers.

Are background checks required?

Yes, background checks are part of our screening process to ensure the safety of our team and the dogs in our care.

What if I can only commit a few hours a month?

That's wonderful! We accept volunteers of all commitment levels. Every bit helps.

How will I be contacted about my application?

We'll contact you via phone or email within 3-5 business days with next steps.

Can I bring my own dog to help socialize the rescue dogs?

We'll evaluate this on a case-by-case basis. Please mention this in your application.
