1. A Fighter Against All Odds
One cold morning, a dog was dumped at our doorstep—broken, bleeding, and clinging to life. He had multiple gunshot wounds and was rushed to the emergency vet. It was a long, painful road to recovery, but this brave soul never gave up. After countless treatments and unwavering care, he healed and found his forever home. Today, he’s living the life he always deserved.

2. The Golden Doodle Miracle
We received a call about a mama Golden Doodle trapped in Goshen. She had been caught in a live trap, terrified and alone. When we arrived, we discovered two tiny puppies with her—and she was still in labor. We brought her to safety, and soon nine beautiful puppies were born. After vetting and care, every single one found a loving home. Mama’s story is a reminder that hope can bloom even in the darkest moments.

3. A Dumpster Walk Turned Nightmare
One February morning, I took my dog Murphy out to the dumpster. Out of nowhere, a 120-pound pit bull attacked. Murphy was thrown into the air, suffering severe injuries that required 47 stitches. I wasn’t spared either—I walked away with 17 stitches, a broken ankle, and a broken leg. It was a terrifying ordeal, but Murphy survived, and so did I. This experience only strengthened our resolve to fight for animals in need.

4. From Mange to Magic
We rescued a dog from a storage building, her body ravaged by severe mange. She had endured unimaginable suffering. Six months of treatment, surgeries, and medication later, she transformed into a healthy, happy pup. Today, she’s thriving with a loving family of three—a true testament to resilience and love.

5. Babies on the Roadside
Partnering with another local rescue, we found a mama dog giving birth on the side of the road. We scooped her and her newborns up and brought them to safety. After vetting and care, every pup went to a warm, loving home. A heartbreaking beginning turned into a beautiful ending.

6. Please, Don’t Dump
We beg people not to abandon animals. Use our front door—we will help. But too often, we find heartbreak waiting outside. One day, a one-day-old kitten with its umbilical cord still attached was left in a Walmart sack at our door. Another time, dogs were tied to trees with Post-it notes saying, “Sorry, I can’t afford to feed. Not my problem anymore.” These moments break us, but they also fuel our mission to protect the voiceless.

7. A Family’s Commitment
For five years, my husband and I, along with our five children, have poured our hearts—and our own savings—into this rescue. Boarding money, Grammy money, every penny goes to vetting these babies. We’ve spent countless nights bottle-feeding orphaned puppies and kittens. This isn’t just a rescue—it’s our life’s calling.

8. The Boxer Mama’s Fight
A few months ago, we rescued a Boxer mama who had given birth under someone’s porch in Booneville. She was gravely ill with cancer. At first, people thought her swollen mammary glands were full of milk—but they weren’t. By the time she arrived, only two puppies were alive. We fought hard, raising funds for her surgery. Sadly, only one puppy survived, but he’s now in an amazing home. Mama is still with us, soaking up love on our couch—a warrior who deserves every moment of peace.
