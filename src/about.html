Our Story

Forgotten Paws of NWA was founded in 2023 in response to the growing crisis of abandoned and at-risk dogs in Northwest Arkansas. Our founders, longtime animal advocates, witnessed firsthand the heartbreaking reality of dogs being surrendered, abandoned, or facing euthanasia simply because they had nowhere to go.

What started as a small foster network has grown into a dedicated shelter in Tontitown, AR, where we provide immediate safety, medical care, and loving attention to dogs who have been overlooked or given up on.

"Every dog deserves dignity, care, and a chance to be loved. We're here to be their bridge from uncertainty to security." — Our Founder

Our Mission & Values

Guiding everything we do

Safe Shelter

Provide immediate, clean, and compassionate shelter for dogs in crisis with no time limit on their stay.

Compassionate Care

Treat every animal with dignity, providing necessary veterinary treatment, nutrition, and emotional support.

Responsible Placement

Ensure each dog is matched with the right forever home through careful screening and support.

Community Partnership

Collaborate with local businesses, veterinarians, and volunteers to ease the burden on our community.

How We Help

Our comprehensive rescue process

1

Rescue & Intake

We accept dogs from animal control, owner surrenders, and rescue abandoned animals. Each dog receives a health assessment and is placed in a clean, comfortable kennel.

2

Medical Care

Every dog is examined by a veterinarian, vaccinated, spayed/neutered, and treated for any medical conditions. We don't cut corners on health.

3

Behavior Assessment

Our team evaluates each dog's temperament, needs, and personality to understand what type of home would be the best fit.

4

Adoption Preparation

Dogs receive basic training, socialization, and grooming to prepare them for life in a loving home.

5

Forever Home Matching

Through our careful adoption process, we match dogs with families who can provide the specific care and environment they need to thrive.

Success Stories

Reggie's Second Chance

Reggie was overlooked for months at animal control and was scheduled to be euthanized. When we took him in, he was shy and unsure. After patient care and socialization at our shelter, Reggie transformed into a confident, loving companion.

Today, he spends his days hiking with his new family and sleeping at the foot of their bed—a living example of why we do what we do.
