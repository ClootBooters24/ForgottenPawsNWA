Volunteer Code of Conduct & Liability Waiver

Effective Date: 8 January 2026

This agreement is between FOR LUV OF FORGOTTEN PAWS (DBA Forgotten Paws of NWA) (the "Organization") and the volunteer ("I," "me," or "Volunteer").

Part 1: Code of Conduct

I agree to abide by the following standards while volunteering:

  • Safety First: I will follow all safety protocols for animal handling, cleaning, and facility use as trained. I will not undertake tasks for which I am not trained.
  • Respect & Professionalism: I will treat all animals, staff, fellow volunteers, and the public with respect and kindness. I will represent the Organization professionally.
  • Animal Welfare: I will follow all instructions regarding feeding, walking, socializing, and medical care. I will report any concerns about an animal's health or behavior immediately.
  • Confidentiality: I will not share private information about adopters, donors, other volunteers, or internal operations.
  • Facility & Property: I will respect the Organization's property and facilities. I am responsible for any damage caused by my negligence.

Failure to adhere to this Code may result in dismissal from the volunteer program.


Part 2: Release of Liability, Waiver, & Indemnification (READ CAREFULLY)

I ACKNOWLEDGE THAT VOLUNTEERING FOR AN ANIMAL RESCUE INVOLVES INHERENT RISKS, including but not limited to animal bites, scratches, zoonotic disease, slips, falls, and injuries from equipment.

In consideration for being allowed to volunteer, I hereby agree to the following:

  1. Assumption of Risk: I voluntarily assume all risks, known and unknown, associated with my volunteer activities.
  2. Release & Waiver: I hereby release, discharge, and hold harmless FOR LUV OF FORGOTTEN PAWS (DBA Forgotten Paws of NWA), its officers, directors, employees, and agents from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising from my volunteer work, whether caused by negligence or otherwise.
  3. Medical Treatment: I authorize the Organization to seek emergency medical treatment for me if necessary. I accept responsibility for all medical costs incurred.
  4. Indemnification: I agree to indemnify (defend and pay) the Organization for any claims or losses resulting from my negligent or intentional acts during volunteer activities.

Part 3: General Provisions

Photographic Release: I grant the Organization permission to use photographs or videos taken of me during volunteer activities for promotional purposes.

At-Will Volunteering: My volunteer status is "at-will" and may be terminated by either party at any time.

Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Arkansas.

Volunteer Certification:
By signing (or electronically submitting), I certify that I have read, understand, and voluntarily agree to all terms of this Code of Conduct and Liability Waiver.
