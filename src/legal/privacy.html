This Privacy Policy describes how [Forgotten Paws of NWA] ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly:

  • When you fill out our volunteer, adoption, or contact forms, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, and any message you provide.

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Like most websites, we collect non-personal data through cookies and analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent. This data is aggregated and anonymous.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and respond to your volunteer or adoption applications.
  • Send you updates about your application or information about our organization (you may opt-out at any time).
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.


3. How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.


4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our website:

  • Web3Forms: Our forms are processed by Web3Forms. Please review their Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: We use this to understand website traffic. Google uses cookies; you can opt-out via Google's Ads Settings.

5. Your Rights (CA/CO/CT/UT/VA residents may have additional rights)

You may contact us to:

  • Request access to or deletion of the personal data we have about you.
  • Opt-out of future communications.
  • Correct inaccurate data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.


7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
