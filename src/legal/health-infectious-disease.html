Health & Infectious Disease Acknowledgment

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Forgotten Paws makes every reasonable effort to ensure animals are healthy and appropriately cared for prior to leaving our care. However, some illnesses and infectious diseases — including parvovirus (parvo), distemper, kennel cough, upper respiratory infections, parasites, and other conditions — may have incubation periods or may not show symptoms at the time of adoption or transfer.

By accepting an animal from Forgotten Paws, I understand that the animal's future health cannot be guaranteed. Once the animal leaves the care and custody of Forgotten Paws, I accept responsibility for the animal's ongoing veterinary care, medical expenses, health monitoring, and treatment.

I understand that Forgotten Paws cannot guarantee that an animal has not previously been exposed to an infectious illness or that symptoms will not develop after leaving the rescue. I agree to promptly seek veterinary care if the animal develops signs of illness.

Vaccinations

Some animals may receive modified-live vaccines as part of their routine vaccination protocol. I understand that vaccination does not provide immediate or guaranteed immunity and that puppies and partially vaccinated animals remain susceptible to infectious diseases while completing their vaccination series. Recent modified-live vaccination may also be relevant when interpreting certain diagnostic test results. I agree to provide my veterinarian with the animal's complete vaccination record and vaccination dates if illness or testing occurs.

Puppies & Unvaccinated/Partially Vaccinated Animals

I understand that young animals may not yet have completed their full vaccination series and may remain susceptible to serious infectious diseases, including parvovirus. Until my veterinarian confirms that the animal is adequately vaccinated, I agree to use appropriate precautions and avoid unnecessary exposure to high-risk environments such as dog parks, pet-store floors, communal dog areas, and areas frequently used by unknown or unvaccinated animals.

I understand that once an animal leaves Forgotten Paws, any subsequent exposure to illness is outside the rescue's control. Any health concerns, veterinary expenses, testing, hospitalization, or treatment arising after the animal leaves Forgotten Paws are my responsibility unless Forgotten Paws has specifically agreed otherwise in writing.

By initialing the adoption or foster application, I acknowledge that I have read and understand this health and infectious disease disclosure and accept responsibility for the animal's care after it leaves Forgotten Paws.
