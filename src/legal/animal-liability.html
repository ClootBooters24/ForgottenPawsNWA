Adoption & Foster Liability Waiver and Code of Conduct

Last Updated: 8 January 2026

IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTICE

This document contains legally binding terms and conditions. By adopting or fostering an animal from Forgotten Paws of NWA, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all terms herein.


1. RELEASE OF LIABILITY AND ASSUMPTION OF RISK

1.1 Acknowledgement of Risks

I, the undersigned Adopter/Foster, acknowledge and understand that:

  • Animals, by their nature, are unpredictable and may cause injury, property damage, or other harm
  • Forgotten Paws of NWA cannot guarantee the health, temperament, or behavior of any animal
  • All animals are adopted/fostered "AS IS" with all faults and risks
  • I assume full responsibility for any and all risks associated with this animal

1.2 Waiver of Claims

I hereby release, discharge, and hold harmless Forgotten Paws of NWA, its directors, officers, volunteers, employees, and agents from any and all claims, demands, actions, or causes of action of any sort, whether at law or in equity, arising from or related to:

  • Any injury to persons or property caused by the animal
  • Any illness, disease, or medical condition of the animal
  • Any behavioral issues or training needs of the animal
  • Any veterinary expenses incurred after adoption/foster transfer
  • Any other damages or losses related to the animal

2. ADOPTION/FOSTER CODE OF CONDUCT

2.1 Animal Welfare Commitment

I agree to provide the adopted/fostered animal with:

  1. Proper Veterinary Care: Immediate examination by a licensed veterinarian within 14 days of adoption/foster, followed by regular preventive care
  2. Appropriate Nutrition: Species-appropriate diet, fresh water daily, and proper feeding schedule
  3. Safe Environment: Secure fencing, proper shelter, and protection from extreme weather conditions
  4. Humane Treatment: No physical punishment, abuse, neglect, or abandonment under any circumstances
  5. Proper Identification: Collar with ID tags at all times; microchip registration updated within 7 days

2.2 Behavioral Management

I agree to:

  • Provide appropriate training using positive reinforcement methods
  • Socialize the animal appropriately with people and other animals
  • Never use choke chains, prong collars, or electronic shock devices
  • Address behavioral issues with professional trainers when necessary
  • Keep the animal leashed or contained when in public spaces

2.3 Legal Compliance

I agree to comply with all applicable laws and regulations including:

  • Local leash laws and animal control ordinances
  • State animal cruelty and neglect statutes
  • Breed-specific legislation where applicable
  • Homeowner's association or rental property restrictions

2.4 Spay/Neuter & No Breeding Policy

Adopter/Foster agrees that all animals will be spayed or neutered at the veterinarian-approved age. If the animal is not already altered at the time of adoption/foster, the Adopter/Foster will arrange for sterilization with a licensed veterinarian at the appropriate age and provide Forgotten Paws with documentation of the procedure within 30 days of completion. If the animal is already spayed or neutered, the Adopter/Foster will provide proof of alteration upon request.

UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES shall the animal be intentionally bred or allowed to reproduce. Intentional breeding, facilitating breeding by others, or permitting the animal to mate is a material breach of this agreement and may result in immediate repossession of the animal, recovery of associated costs, reporting to animal welfare authorities, and permanent disqualification from future adoptions or fosters.


3. SPECIFIC FOSTER RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Foster-Specific Requirements

As a foster caregiver, I additionally agree to:

  1. Provide regular updates (photos, videos, behavioral notes) to Forgotten Paws at least weekly
  2. Transport the animal to scheduled veterinary appointments and adoption events
  3. Allow prospective adopters to visit the animal in my home (with advance notice)
  4. Return the animal to Forgotten Paws immediately upon request for any reason
  5. Not re-home, transfer, or give away the animal to any other person or organization

3.2 Medical Care for Foster Animals

I understand that:

  • Forgotten Paws covers approved veterinary expenses for foster animals
  • I must obtain PRIOR authorization for any non-emergency veterinary care
  • Emergency situations require immediate care followed by notification within 24 hours
  • I am responsible for administering medications as prescribed

4. RETURN POLICY & NON-COMPLIANCE

4.1 Return Requirements

I agree that if I can no longer care for the animal for any reason:

  • I will return the animal ONLY to Forgotten Paws of NWA
  • I will provide at least 72 hours notice when possible
  • I understand there may be a waiting period for intake depending on available space
  • I will provide all medical records and supplies with the returned animal

4.2 Consequences of Non-Compliance

I understand that failure to comply with this agreement may result in:

  1. Immediate repossession of the animal by Forgotten Paws
  2. Legal action to recover the animal and associated costs
  3. Reporting to appropriate animal welfare authorities
  4. Permanent disqualification from future adoptions/fostering

5. ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS & SIGNATURES

5.1 Final Acknowledgements

By signing below, I certify that:

  • I have read and understand this entire document
  • All information I have provided is true and complete
  • I am at least 21 years of age
  • I have the financial means to properly care for this animal
  • No members of my household have allergies to this type of animal
  • All members of my household agree to this adoption/foster arrangement
