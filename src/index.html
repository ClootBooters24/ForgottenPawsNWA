Testimonial

Meet Reggie! He was overlooked for months at animmal control and was scheduled to be euthanized. Forgotten Paws immediately stepped up to help, giving him a safe place to land. Reggie has since found his forever home. Forgotten Paws gives dogs like Reggie the second chance they deserve.
